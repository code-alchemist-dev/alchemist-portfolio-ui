Privacy

Your information on this site

This site is run by Bright Mabuza (Code Alchemist, Alchemia) in South Africa. It is a portfolio, so it collects very little.

What is collected

The contact form. If you send a message, your name, email, topic and message are stored by the site host, Netlify, so that I can read and answer it. I use them only to reply to you, never for marketing, and I don’t share them. Once our conversation is finished I delete the submission. To have it deleted sooner, or to ask what I hold, contact me through the form or on LinkedIn.

No tracking. The site sets no cookies, loads nothing from other companies, and runs no analytics or advertising scripts.

Server logs. Like any web host, Netlify processes basic request data (such as IP address and browser) to deliver the site and keep it secure, under its own privacy policy.

Your rights

Under South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act you may ask to see, correct or delete the information I hold about you, and you may complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa).

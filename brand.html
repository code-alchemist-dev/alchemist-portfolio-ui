Brand

Code Alchemist · Alchemia

Code Alchemist is Bright Mabuza’s professional identity, and Alchemia is the name of his independent practice. The mark puts code brackets inside the alchemical triangle for fire, the element of transformation, enclosed in the circle of the Great Work. The gold point above is the spark: the moment a problem turns into a solution.

Using it

Do

  • Use the gold mark on Ink, or the dark lockup on Cream.
  • Keep the mark’s proportions and clear space.
  • Pair “Code Alchemist” with “Bright Mabuza” wherever people need the real name.

Don’t

  • Recolour, stretch, rotate or add effects to the mark.
  • Put the gold mark on busy photos or mid-tone backgrounds.
  • Use the brand to imply a client relationship that doesn’t exist.
